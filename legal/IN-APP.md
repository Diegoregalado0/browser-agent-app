# In-product text

Short texts for the extension UI, in Browsby's voice. These are proposals, except the policy links, which are in Settings > Data and privacy.

## Disclaimer (onboarding, last setup step, or Settings > General)

> Browsby acts in your tabs with your sign-ins, so what it does, it does as you. It can make mistakes: watch tasks that matter, and press Stop if it goes the wrong way.

Shorter, for tight spaces:

> The agent acts with your access and can make mistakes. Watch tasks that matter.

## Data disclosure (setup, on the provider or key step)

> To do a task, Browsby sends your request and what it sees on the page, including screenshots, to the provider you pick. Nothing is sent to us. With a local model, it stays on your computer.

## Links (Settings > Data and privacy, and the setup footer)

> Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Settings > Data and privacy links to https://diegoregalado0.github.io/browser-agent-app/legal/PRIVACY and .../legal/TERMS (GitHub Pages, not turned on yet). The setup footer does not have them yet.

## Auto mode warning (next to the Auto option, if you want it firmer)

> In Auto, nothing checks the agent's actions, and a page can trick it into doing something you did not ask for.
