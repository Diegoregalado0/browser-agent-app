# Positioning and voice

The product is **Browsby**, a Chrome extension. Say "Browsby" for the product and "the agent" for what does the work. Brand details are in BRAND.md.

## One-line promise

Say what you want done, and Browsby does it in your own Chrome tabs, on your own AI key, with nothing sent through us.

## Three benefits

1. **It does the clicking.** Describe a task in plain words; the agent opens pages, clicks, types, fills in forms, and tells you when it is finished. You can watch every step and press Stop at any time.
2. **Your key, your provider, no middleman.** Bring a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Mistral, or use a local model through Ollama. The key stays on your computer and goes only to that provider. No account with us, no server of ours in between.
3. **It asks before it matters.** A safety check compares each action with what you asked for and watches pages for text that tries to take over. On banks, payment sites, password managers, and password fields it always asks first.

Keep Chrome. Browsby is an extension for the browser you already use; there is no new browser to install.

## Voice rules

- Plain, calm, trustworthy: a careful colleague explaining what will happen.
- Product first, then detail. Lead with what the user can do, not how it is built.
- Short sentences, everyday words. Second person ("your tabs", "your key").
- Say what it does and what it does not do, concretely: "The key is sent only to your provider."
- When it stops or asks, say why in one line.
- No em dashes: use a comma, a colon, or a new sentence.
- No exclamation marks, no emojis in product copy.

## Words to avoid

- Hype: revolutionary, supercharge, magic, magical, 10x, game-changing, seamless, effortless, unleash, next-generation, cutting-edge.
- Vague AI talk: "AI-powered" as a lead, "copilot", "autopilot", "assistant that thinks for you", "sentient", "understands you".
- Overpromising: "anything", "fully autonomous", "never makes mistakes", "100% private", "secure" without saying how.
- Jargon up front: CDP, LLM, tokens, agentic (fine in developer sections, not in the first lines).

Prefer: task, tabs, your key, your provider, asks first, stop, on your computer, nothing sent to us.
